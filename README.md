A program that draws one 1024×1024 image summing up the world news of
Friday, 27 November 2026, as a newspaper front page: a masthead with the
date, one lead story, four stories in a grid, and a closing strip.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png`. No network, no clock, no randomness: running
it twice writes byte-identical output.

## The six stories

1. **Trade** — the US proposes tariffs of up to 25% on Indian goods
   (17% if Delhi signs a trade deal) after a probe of excess capacity
   in 16 economies. (USTR via Reuters / Onmanorama)
2. **North Korea** — Kim Jong Un tours nuclear-material plants and calls
   the arsenal irreversible, spurning Trump's offer of talks. (KCNA via
   Associated Press)
3. **Space** — Elon Musk pledges two SpaceX shares for every child in
   the US, one from the company and one from himself, worth about $18bn.
   (Reuters)
4. **Egypt** — a fuel tanker overturns on Cairo's ring road and bursts
   into flames; 31 dead, 21 hurt. (Egypt health ministry via AFP)
5. **Thailand** — an explosion at Bangchak's Phra Khanong refinery in
   Bangkok hurts seven, four critically, and halts all units. (Channel
   News Asia / Bangkok Post)
6. **Europe** — EU leaders split over a near-€1.6tn budget and a $49bn
   loan for Ukraine as Russian strikes kill five. (Al Jazeera)

All pictograms are drawn in code with plain shapes; no downloaded images
are used.
